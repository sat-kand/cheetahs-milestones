Matches, wickets and runs are career totals for Cheetahs matches across all seasons, the same numbers as the club statistics page. A player counts as reaching a milestone when the matches, wickets or runs in the period lift the total to it or over it, so someone on 164 wickets only appears if they were below 150 before the period started. A two-day match counts on the day it finishes.

Players with no Cheetahs match in the last 8 months are treated as having left and are left out of the watchlist. The Team column shows the grade a player is playing in this season; a second grade is listed only if it is a regular, not an occasional fill-in.
