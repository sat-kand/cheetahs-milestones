Match counts come from the batting statistics, so they follow the same numbers as the club page. "Next game" means the player has played one match fewer than the milestone.

Wickets are career totals for Cheetahs matches across all seasons. A player counts as crossing a milestone when the wickets taken in the period lift the total over it, so someone on 164 only appears if they were below 150 before the period started. A two-day match counts on the day it finishes.
